1. Overview & Objective

Executive Marketing & Web Analytics Dashboards

This collection showcases 4 key Google Looker Studio reports engineered for cross-channel marketing tracking, audience behavior analysis, sales conversions, and business health monitoring.

2. Report Breakdown & Features

Report 01 — Cross-Channel Marketing ROI

  • Consolidates paid ad performance across Google Ads, Meta Ads, and LinkedIn.
  • Tracks total spend, customer acquisition costs (CAC), and overall ROAS.

Report 02 — Executive Revenue & Sales Pipeline

  • Presents high-level business revenue trends, profit margins, and deal win rates.
  • Includes dynamic date-range filtration and regional performance maps.

Report 03 — Website Traffic & User Journeys

  • Integrates directly with Google Analytics 4 (GA4) event streams.
  • Evaluates session durations, landing page bounce rates, and conversion funnels.

Report 04 — E-commerce & Conversion Performance

  • Detailed funnel analytics mapping cart additions, checkouts, and completed orders.
  • Analyzes customer retention and repeat purchase frequencies over time.

3. Key Technologies Used

  • Data Connectors: Google Analytics 4, BigQuery, Google Sheets, PostgreSQL.
  • Calculated Fields: Custom REGEX extraction, CASE WHEN logic, and custom metrics.
  • Design Standards: Custom color palettes matching enterprise design systems.
